## How it's built

- **Months are a `Scale` of intervals**: ticks every 4 weeks, each slat a Bar from `d.at` to `d.next`, every other one shaded.
- **Today is a second Plot** drawn over the trades, with `pointer-events: none` so the pointer reaches the trades under it.
- **Hover a trade** to see it dimensioned, as on a drawing: a line with end marks and its length in weeks.
