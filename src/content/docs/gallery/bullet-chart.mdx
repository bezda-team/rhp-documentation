## How it's built

- **Three blocks per goal.** A track Bar to 120, the progress Bar, and a white Tick at the goal (100).
- **Icons in the gutter.** SVG paths in the start Label, colored by the row's `--rhp-color`.
- **Hover a goal** for what it comes to in its own unit, in a bubble at the tip of the progress.
