## How it's built

- **An overlap Plot of Dots** per group, one per patient, each drawn as a capsule.
- **Fixed jitter.** A patient's place across the band and its tilt are fixed functions of its index, so they stay put when the data changes.
- **The average** is a Tick, with its number in the end gutter.
