## How it's built

- **A day per slat**, holding a Plot with `orientation="across"` of 24 Cells: the hours run across the day's band.
- **Color by CSS.** A Cell mixes the theme's `low` and `high` colors by its value on the chart's scale.
- **Hover a cell** for its title. The heatmap keeps rhp's plain look, in the page's colors.
