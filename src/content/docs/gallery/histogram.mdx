## How it's built

- **`bins()`** over 365 days gives `x0`, `x1` and `tally` per bin, three data groups for the Plot.
- **Color from the bin.** Each Bar takes its temperature's color from a cool-to-hot ramp, shown in the key.
- **Hover a bar** for its count of days. Horizontal puts the hottest bins on top, like a thermometer.
