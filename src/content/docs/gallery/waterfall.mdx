## How it's built

- **`running()`** turns the month's amounts into `from` and `to` for each step; a hairline Tick at each step's end links it to the next.
- **Click an expense to cut it.** An expense's name is a button, and the poster listens for clicks. A signal of cut rows feeds `running()`, so the later steps, the savings and the headline figure all move together.
- **A cut keeps its place.** The step has no length, and a dashed Bar shows where it was.
