## How it's built

- **Shares, stacked.** `stackUp(shares(d.use))` turns each person's minutes into segments of 100, drawn inside a shell Bar a little larger than the track, with a Tick for the nub.
- **Numbers only where they fit.** A segment under 9% hides its number; on a narrow battery (a container query) the cut is 16%.
- **Point at an app**, in the key or in a battery, or click to keep it; a click anywhere else, or Escape, lets it go. The poster turns the pointer and the clicks into one app, passed to the Plot as `focus`: every battery lights that app and fades the rest.
