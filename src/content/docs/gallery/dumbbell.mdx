## How it's built

- **A route and two markers.** A 3px Bar from camp to summit, with the tent and the peak drawn as Dots clipped to triangles.
- **Real peaks.** Summit heights from recent surveys; base camps are the usual ones, and vary by route.
- **Re-sort by the climb.** Rows are keyed by peak, so each climb keeps its slat and slides to its new place.
