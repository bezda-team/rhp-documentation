## How it's built

- **A Plot inside a slat.** Each team is a slat, and the slat holds a Plot of three counts: gold, silver and bronze. The inner Plot fills its team's band (`thick={0.86}` of it) and splits it three ways.
- **A ribbon and a medal.** A count is a 3px Bar from 0 and a Dot at its value, with the number struck on the medal's face.
- **Ranked like a medal table.** `sortBy` puts the most golds first, then silvers, then bronzes. Rows are keyed by name, so a team keeps its slat when the order changes and slides to its new place.
- **Hover a count.** The medal lifts off the table: its face is an element inside the Dot, so it can grow and cast a shadow on its own time while rhp moves the Dot.
