- **`density()`** feeds a mirrored Area per instrument, with one peak for all four, so their widths compare.
- **The pitch scale is a keyboard**: a `Scale` with a slat per semitone, each key a Bar from `d.at` to `d.next`.
- **Point at an instrument.** Its range goes to the Scale as data (`low`, `high`, `tint`), and the keys it reaches light up in its varnish.
