- **Rows as objects.** `rows={days()}` passes one object per day; the slat reads `d.open`, `d.close` and so on.
- **A fitted scale.** `nice()` rounds the month's range, and its ticks label the axis.
- **Point at a day.** A second Plot of zero or one slat draws a dashed line at its close and its price over the axis, and the readout shows its open, high, low and close.
