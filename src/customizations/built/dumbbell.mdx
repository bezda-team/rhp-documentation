- **A route and two markers.** A 3px Bar from camp to summit, with the tent and the peak drawn as Dots clipped to triangles.
- **Real peaks.** Summit heights from recent surveys; base camps are the usual ones, and vary by route.
- **Re-sort by the climb.** Two buttons under the dek pick the order. Rows are keyed by peak, so each climb keeps its slat and slides to its new place.
- **The climb on the route.** Ordered by the climb, each summit's height fades out and a Label at the middle of the route fades in with the climb: under the line, or on it and turned along it when vertical. The slat root's class, from the order passed to the Plot, switches them.
