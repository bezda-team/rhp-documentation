- **Two Bars per band.** Men from `-(value + SPINE)` to `-SPINE`, women from `SPINE` to `value + SPINE`, so the ages fit on a spine between them.
- **Order is data.** Horizontal, a list of positions puts the oldest band on top, like a pyramid; vertical keeps data order.
- **The scale fits the widest band** of either year, rounded up, on both sides, so the two years compare.
- **1990 or 2024.** The two years are buttons in the poster's key; one swaps the Plot's data, and each band's Bars move to that year's shares.
