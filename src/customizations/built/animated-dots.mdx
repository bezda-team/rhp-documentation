- **Slats of dots.** Each slat holds an overlap Plot of 30 Dots, lit or not from a line of the logo's art (`#` is lit).
- **A window, not a canvas.** The scale is 11 dots wide and each slat clips its dots to it (`overflow-x: clip` in the slat's CSS), so a slat can shift 4 dots one way or 5 the other and the window stays full.
- **Motion is data.** Every 5 seconds a signal gives the middle slats new shifts, and the next time it puts them back. Hover the window to hold the logo; **New data** scatters it now.
