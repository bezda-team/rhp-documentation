- **`stackUp(d.ml)`** in a memo per drink gives each layer its `from` and `to`; an overlap Plot lays the layers on one band.
- **A shared data group** tells the last layer with anything in it to round the cup's end, and an empty layer hides.
- **Hover a layer** to lift it and read its pour, from a tag inside the layer's Bar. The drink under the pointer comes over the others, since slats paint in data order.
