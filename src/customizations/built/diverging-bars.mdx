The scale runs from -10 to 10, so zero sits in the middle and a Bar drawn to a negative value runs the other way by itself.
The slat contains three blocks: a start `Label` for the month, a `Bar` to `d.change`, and a `Label` at that same value.
`side="before"` moves that label to the other side of the bar's end when the change is negative, so the number always sits outside the bar rather than on top of it.

The gallery version adds:

- **A colour taken from the value.**
  Each month's Bar picks a colour from a blue-to-red ramp, and the same colour tints the slat through a CSS variable.
- **The axis is drawn by a `Scale`.**
  A hairline at every degree, with zero brighter than the rest and named "normal".
- **Pointing at a month fades the others.**
  The month under the pointer goes back into the Plot as data, so each slat decides for itself whether to fade.
